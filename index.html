What's inside

Everything a friend group needs, in one orbit.

Real screens from Astro, running on sample friends.

Orbit

Your friends, in orbit.

Everyone who's free today drifts to the inner ring. Tap anyone to message them, tap your own planet to share what you're up to.

Chat

Group chat that pulls its weight.

Photos, videos, voice notes, GIFs, replies and reactions, with the plan and the bill pinned right in the thread.

Plans and RSVPs

From “we should” to a real plan.

Make the plan, see who’s going, get a reminder before it starts. Friends without the app can RSVP from a link.

Signal

Anyone free?

Send a signal to the friends you pick. Their answers land on their planets, and one tap turns the Ins into a plan.

Wallet

Split the bill, get paid back.

Even splits or by item, with a Venmo or Zelle handoff to settle up. Astro never touches your money.

Comms

Open a line, not a call.

Voice straight between the people in the chat. Ring a friend, or leave the line open while you game.

Customize

Your planet, your look.

Pick a color and look, add a photo, choose a theme and the sounds you hear. Friends see it in their orbit.

Astro Plus

Rings, moons, skies and more room.

Planet styles, sky effects, sound packs, a bigger orbit, Orbit Recap and Crew Pass for the whole group. Free while Astro is in beta.

Private by default

Built for your friends, not for an audience.

No feed, no followers, nothing to perform. Just the people you already hang out with.

  • Your status clears on its own
  • Friends never see your exact location
  • No public profile or follower count
  • No ads in your chats, ever
  • Your group's data is never sold

Bring your crew into orbit.

Sign in with your email, make a group, and send your crew the invite code.
