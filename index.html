What you get

Three things your group text can't do.

Tables

A private home for each group: the boys, the fantasy league, poker night. Its own chat, its own look, its own history.

Tonight

Tap free, gaming, or going out. Your friends see it until it expires on its own. When three of you are free, Roundtable asks if you want to start something.

Plans

Turn any message into a plan. Going, maybe, or can't in one tap, a vote when nobody can agree on a time, and reminders so people show up.

Built for your friends, not for an audience.

  • No public profile or follower count
